# production-v1.14.0 (N5.1 + N6.2) — full L4 scorecard, 15 November 2026

Decision input: keep or reject `native_spec` (configs/profiles/production-v1.14.0.toml) against
`native_dattn` (v1.13), with vLLM and SGLang re-measured in the same run.

- **v1.13** = v1.12 plus the N5.1 split-KV GQA decode attention kernel in the static lane.
- **v1.14** = v1.13 plus N6.2: lossless one-draft speculative decoding with the checkpoint's own
  MTP head.
  - The GDN recurrent state is banked: a rejected draft flips a selector instead of copying.
  - Drafts use the first 65536 token ids.
  - The MTP history comes from the last 256 prefill columns.
  - Greedy rows only.

Regressions:

1. **TTFT under load:** interactive c4 71 → 91 ms, c8 94 → 126 ms, c16 107 → 193 ms; moderate c8
   265 → 293 ms. Spec admission is per row and eager: `load_spec_row` runs one MTP prefill and
   several host syncs per row.
2. **prefill_heavy c4 E2E p95:** 1122 → 1363 ms (decode ms/tok 19.2 → 21.8). The spec step costs
   more than a plain step, and prefill-heavy rows spend little time decoding.

## Where native still trails

- **TTFT at every load.** At c1, interactive is 65 ms against 41 for both rivals, and moderate is
  125 ms against 45–48.
- **prefill_heavy c4–c16 and shared_prefix c16.**
  - Decode ms/tok there is 22–67 (SGLang 17–22).
  - Prefill forwards still stall every decoding row.
  - SGLang also reuses the shared prefix (radix cache) and keeps TTFT at 122–168 ms.

## Decision

- **Keep v1.14 (`native_spec`) as the production profile.** Throughput improves in every cell, and
  the remaining regressions are TTFT/E2E, with a known cause.
- Next, in order:
  1. **N6.3:** batch the spec admission (one MTP prefill for all admitted rows, no per-row syncs),
     to win back the TTFT added at c4–c16.
  2. **N5.2:** mixed prefill+decode forwards, aimed at prefill_heavy c4–c16 and shared_prefix c16.
  3. **TTFT at c1** (65 vs 41 ms): profile the single-request path (prefill launch overhead,
     first-token sampling sync).
