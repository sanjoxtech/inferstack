## Findings

1. **v1.12 against v1.9.** Prefill-bound cells improve and none is outside noise in the other
   direction. prefill_heavy c16: tok/s ×1.28, TTFT 2210 → 794 ms, E2E p95 5867 → 4161 ms.
   prefill_heavy c8: ×1.14, TTFT 911 → 667 ms. moderate c16: TTFT 651 → 608 ms.
2. **Against the rivals, native trails in every cell:** ×0.82–0.92 at low load, ×0.46–0.51 at
   prefill_heavy c16 and shared_prefix c16. Prefill_heavy c16 TTFT is now 794 ms (vLLM 468).
3. **Batched decode is now the largest gap.** At c1 native is 17.0 vs 15.8 ms/token. Under load
   the decode time per token grows much faster than vLLM's:
   - interactive c16 (almost pure decode): 24.7 vs 19.4;
   - shared_prefix c16: 50.5 vs 21.3;
   - prefill_heavy c16: 86.2 vs 30.7.

   Two causes:
   - The static-lane decode step itself is slower at batch 16.
   - Every prefill forward stalls all decoding rows. vLLM and SGLang put decode tokens into the
     same forward as the prefill chunk.

## Decision

- **Keep v1.12 (`native_drain`) as the production profile.** v1.10 and v1.11 were intermediate
  candidates.
- Next, in order:
  1. Measure the static-lane decode step at batch 4/8/16 against the bandwidth floor
     (interactive c16 gap).
  2. Mixed prefill+decode forwards, so prefill no longer stalls decoding rows (shared_prefix and
     prefill_heavy c16 gaps).
  3. Packed/unpadded attention for prefill (117 ms of masked mem-efficient attention at B=16).
