## v1.15 against v1.14

- **TTFT:**
  - Better in 13 of 16 cells, often by 2–3×. Examples: prefill_heavy c16 894 → 350, moderate c16
    434 → 157.
  - Worse in 3 cells: interactive c1 +7 ms, shared_prefix c1 +11 ms, shared_prefix c16 +28 ms.
- **Output tok/s:**
  - Better in 6 cells, with non-overlapping ranges. Examples: prefill_heavy c16 +26%, c8 +23%;
    moderate c16 +9%.
  - About equal in 7 cells.
  - **Worse on shared_prefix c4, c8 and c16** (−12%, −15%, −5%). The cause is client decode
    ms/token rising at c4 (13.6 → 18.5) and c8 (23.0 → 29.2): prefills of arriving requests now
    run at admission and interrupt decode more often.

## Against the rivals (v1.15)

- **Output tok/s:** v1.15 leads both vLLM and SGLang in 11 of 16 cells. This is all of
  interactive and moderate, plus prefill_heavy c1 and shared_prefix c1 and c4. The rivals lead
  prefill_heavy c4–c16 and shared_prefix c8–c16.
- **TTFT:**
  - v1.15 is fastest at interactive c4 and c16.
  - SGLang still leads most prefill-bound cells.
  - At c1 the rivals' 43–54 ms is still ahead of our 73–137 ms. That gap is per-forward
    overhead, which P4 targets.

## Decision

**Keep v1.15.** It improves TTFT across nearly the whole scorecard and raises throughput where
prefill dominates. The regression is confined to shared_prefix decode throughput at c4–c16. Next:

1. **Shared_prefix decode interference.** Pace packed admissions while decode rows are live,
   the P3 scheduling step.
2. **P4 prefill graphs.** For the c1 TTFT gap.
