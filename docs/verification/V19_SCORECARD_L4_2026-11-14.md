## Findings

1. **Token-budget prefill (v1.8)** cuts TTFT in every prefill-bound cell (moderate c1
   238 → 127 ms, prefill_heavy c8 1572 → 918 ms) and raises tok/s ×1.15–×1.24 in
   prefill_heavy. One regression: shared_prefix c16 E2E p95 3114 → 3678 ms (+18%) with
   decode 48.3 → 50.6 ms/tok. The busy budget (1024 tokens per forward while decoding) slows
   the decoding rows when many prefix-hit requests arrive together.
2. **Direct FLA (v1.9)** beats Transformers-dispatched FLA where single requests prefill
   long prompts: prefill_heavy c1 TTFT 344 → 187 ms, c16 2534 → 2212 ms, tok/s ×1.11, and
   decode at c16 83.7 → 75.0 ms/tok. No cell is outside the noise in the other direction.
3. **Against the rivals, native still trails in every cell** on tok/s: ×0.79–0.93 at
   interactive, falling to ×0.36–0.41 at prefill_heavy c16. Two gaps remain:
   - batched decode: 16.9 vs 15.8 ms/tok at c1, but 24.4 vs 19.4 at interactive c16 and
     50.9 vs 21.5 at shared_prefix c16;
   - batched prefill: at prefill_heavy c16, TTFT is 2212 ms vs 476 (vLLM) and 162 (SGLang).
     Prefill rows still run one padded job at a time.

## Decision

- **Keep v1.9 (`native_fla`) as the production profile**, with v1.8 as the fallback when FLA
  is not installed (install-time check raises if `gdn_prefill_backend = "fla"` and FLA is
  missing).
- Follow-ups in order:
  1. N4 packed/varlen prefill (FLA `cu_seqlens`), aimed at prefill_heavy c8/c16;
  2. busy-budget tuning (512) for the shared_prefix c16 tail;
  3. batched decode efficiency at c ≥ 8.
