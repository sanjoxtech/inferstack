# InferStack positioning (2026-11-19)

## One line

InferStack finds, proves and keeps current the cheapest setup that meets your SLO for your model, your
hardware and your traffic, across engines (ours, vLLM, SGLang) and, with Model Foundry, across model
variants, measured as cost per successful task.

## The market we are not entering

| player | strength | why we do not fight it head-on |
|---|---|---|
| vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM | breadth: every model, every feature, large teams | one team cannot match coverage; on chat at 12 req/s vLLM beats us today (12 vs 8 req/s goodput on L4) |
| GPU clouds and inference APIs (Together, Fireworks, Baseten...) | scale, capacity, private tuning teams | capital and hardware we do not have |
| config tuners (SCOOT, NVIDIA AIConfigurator, Novita autotuner) | knob search inside one engine | they stop at settings: no engine choice, no code changes, no model changes, no memory across customers |
| self-improving agents (Darwin Gödel Machine, ShinkaEvolve, autokernel) | open-ended program improvement | research systems, not a serving decision with SLOs, costs and rollbacks |

## Our edge (all four together)

1. **Engine-neutral and honest.** The output is the measured winner per (model, hardware, workload), even
   when it is a rival. Every claim ships with reproducible evidence, failures included (P9-P13: 13
   experiments, 6 rejected, 2 stopped at a cheap gate, all published). In a market full of benchmark games
   and reward hacking, a verifier the optimizer cannot touch is the product.
2. **Memory that compounds.** Every run is stored with its model, hardware, workload, SLO, settings,
   engine counters and decision. A new deployment starts from what is already known to work or fail
   there; tuning gets cheaper with each customer. Tricks are copyable; this dataset is not.
3. **Day-0 speed on new architectures.** A native fast path for new model families (hybrid Gated DeltaNet
   + attention, MTP heads) plus the agent loop. On Qwen3.5-2B, L4: ties vLLM on 500- and 2k-token prompts,
   beats SGLang in every case, 45 ms first token at light load; lossless one-draft MTP speculative
   decoding, lazy GDN state banks, pool prefix cache for hybrid state.
4. **Model + serving co-optimisation (unique).** Model Foundry changes the model (quantisation,
   distillation, weight edits), InferStack changes the serving, FactoryOS measures task success. The
   objective becomes **$ per successful task at the SLO**, not tokens/s. Engines cannot change the model;
   model shops do not touch serving.

## Wedge customer

Teams running agents on small open models on low-cost GPUs (L4, T4, A10, later consumer and edge
hardware): cost per task matters more than peak throughput, and nobody tunes the model and the serving
together for them. FactoryOS is customer zero.

## Proof we need (in order)

1. The loop runs by itself: harness gaps G1-G6 ([AUTO_IMPROVE_HARNESS_DESIGN.md](AUTO_IMPROVE_HARNESS_DESIGN.md)).
2. A public, reproducible table per (model, GPU, workload): winner engine, settings, goodput, $/1k
   requests, evidence links; regenerated by the harness, not by hand.
3. Edge 4 demonstrated once: a Foundry-tuned model on a tuned InferStack setup beats the stock model on
   vLLM on $ per successful FactoryOS task, same SLO, same GPU, held-out tasks.
4. A second hardware type (Colab T4 or A100) tuned from scratch by the harness, no hand edits.

## What we say no to (for now)

Multi-node and disaggregated serving, every model family, our own GPU cloud, chasing peak tokens/s
headlines, FP8 or other numerics changes presented as like-for-like with BF16 rivals.
